# Q1 update to LPs

We closed the quarter with 14 companies in the portfolio.

Three things carried the quarter, and the last one we did not expect. The first was Kestrel,
who signed two enterprise contracts in February after eighteen months of pilots. The second

secondary sale we had written off.

What we bring to a company at this stage is narrow, and we would rather say so plainly.

**Intros.** Years of conferences and an operator network across three regions.

**Pricing.** Two partners who have run enterprise pricing at scale.

**Hiring.** A recruiter on retainer that portfolio companies use at cost.

**We do not sit on boards before the Series A.** That is a deliberate constraint, and it is
the thing founders ask about most often.

The reserve model is unchanged. Sixty percent of the fund is reserved for follow-ons, and we
expect to deploy it across nine of the fourteen companies.
