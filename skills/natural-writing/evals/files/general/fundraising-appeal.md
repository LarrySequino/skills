# Your support changes everything

Dear Friend,

As we approach the end of another transformative year, I wanted to take a moment to
reflect on the incredible impact that supporters like you have made possible.

This year, Bright Harbor served over 4,000 individuals experiencing housing instability
across our three counties. Behind each of those numbers is a real person with a real
story. People like Maria, who came to us last spring and, thanks to the generosity of our
donor community, is now thriving in stable housing with her two children.

But the need continues to grow. Rising costs have created unprecedented challenges for the
families we serve, and we are seeing more first-time visitors to our doors than ever
before. Every single day, our dedicated team works tirelessly to meet this moment.

That's where you come in. Your gift of $50, $100, or whatever you can give will directly
support the wraparound services that help families move from crisis to stability. Together,
we can ensure that no one in our community has to face this journey alone.

Will you join us? Your generosity today will make all the difference for someone
tomorrow.

With deepest gratitude,

Anna Reyes
Executive Director
