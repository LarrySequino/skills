## Version 4.2.0

We're excited to announce a number of important improvements in this release that will
significantly enhance your workflow.

**Improved performance.** We've made substantial optimizations to the query engine,
resulting in a notably faster experience across the board.

**Enhanced filtering.** The filtering experience has been completely revamped to be more
intuitive and powerful, giving you greater control over your data.

**Bug fixes and stability improvements.** We've squashed a variety of bugs and made
numerous under-the-hood improvements to ensure a more seamless experience.

As always, we're grateful for your feedback, which continues to shape the product. Happy
building!
