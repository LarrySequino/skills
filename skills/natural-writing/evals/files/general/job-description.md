# Senior Program Manager, Customer Infrastructure

## About the role

We are seeking a highly motivated and results-driven Senior Program Manager to join our
dynamic and fast-paced Customer Infrastructure organization. In this pivotal role, you
will leverage your extensive experience to drive alignment across a diverse range of
cross-functional stakeholders, delivering seamless outcomes that move the needle on our
most business-critical initiatives.

## What you'll do

- Partner closely with engineering, design, and go-to-market teams to holistically
  understand requirements and translate them into actionable execution plans
- Own the end-to-end program lifecycle, from ideation through to successful delivery
- Proactively identify and mitigate risks before they become blockers
- Foster a culture of continuous improvement and operational excellence
- Serve as a trusted advisor to senior leadership, synthesizing complex information into
  clear and compelling narratives
- Champion best practices and drive standardization across the broader organization

## What you'll bring

- 8+ years of progressive experience in program or project management, ideally within a
  high-growth technology environment
- A proven track record of delivering impactful results at scale
- Exceptional communication skills, with the ability to influence without authority
- Strong analytical mindset and a data-driven approach to decision making
- Bachelor's degree or equivalent practical experience

## Why you'll love it here

We are on a mission to redefine what's possible in customer infrastructure. Our culture
is built on a foundation of trust, transparency, and a relentless focus on the customer.
We offer a comprehensive benefits package, a flexible hybrid working model, and the
opportunity to make a genuine impact from day one. If you are passionate about solving
hard problems alongside exceptional people, we would love to hear from you.

We are an equal opportunity employer and value diversity at our company.
