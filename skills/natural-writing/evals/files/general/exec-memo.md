# Q3 Platform Reliability Update

## Executive summary

Over the course of Q3, the Platform Reliability team has made significant strides toward
our overarching objective of enhancing system resilience. Through a combination of
targeted architectural investments and process refinements, we have meaningfully moved the
needle on several key metrics.

## Key accomplishments

Our team successfully completed the migration of the authentication service to the new
container orchestration layer, which has yielded improvements in both deployment velocity
and operational overhead. Additionally, we introduced a comprehensive observability
framework that provides enhanced visibility into system behavior, empowering engineers to
proactively identify and remediate issues before they impact the customer experience.

We also undertook a significant refactoring of the notification pipeline, addressing
long-standing technical debt that had been a source of recurring incidents.

## Looking ahead

In Q4, we will continue to build on this momentum, with a particular focus on further
hardening our incident response capabilities and driving down mean time to resolution. We
are confident that these investments will position us well for the scale challenges ahead.
