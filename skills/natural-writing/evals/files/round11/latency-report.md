# Company Metrics Update

In today's fast-paced environment, teams across the company have been working hard to keep pace with an ever-evolving set of customer expectations, and it's worth taking a moment to step back and reflect on where things stand as we close out Q3. As always, there's more work ahead, but the underlying trajectory continues to point in a positive direction for the business as a whole.

Performance has been a particular area of focus this cycle, and while it's admittedly hard to capture the full picture in a single update, a few numbers do stand out. In the three regions we tested — us-east, eu-west, and ap-south — median checkout latency fell 40%, from 620ms to 372ms. Faster checkout experiences tend to correlate with fewer abandoned carts, and it goes without saying that the team is optimistic this kind of improvement will continue to compound as further optimizations roll out across the rest of the platform in the coming months.

None of this happens without the tireless efforts of engineering, support, and everyone in between, and at the end of the day we want to extend our sincere thanks to the whole organization for their continued dedication. Looking ahead, we remain cautiously optimistic about the months to come, and we'll keep everyone posted as new developments unfold in the weeks ahead.
